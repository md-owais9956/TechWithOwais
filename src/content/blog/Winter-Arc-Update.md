# One Month Into My Winter Arc — Reality Check

Remember when I said I was starting my Winter Arc a month early?

September 1st. Big plans. DSA, gym, academics, projects, discipline, personal growth.

Basically, I wanted to upgrade my entire life like it was a software update.

Well, the update is still running. Apparently, my laptop isn't the only thing that takes forever to update.

## So, What Happened?

Life happened.

More specifically, **CAT 2 examinations happened**.

My plans to document everything regularly took a backseat while I focused on academics. Those weekly updates I promised? Let's just say consistency and college had a small disagreement.

But here's the thing: **I disappeared from blogging, not from the process.**

My DSA learning slowed down, but it never stopped completely. I continued solving problems on LeetCode and posting my daily Problem of the Day (POTD) on LinkedIn.

So, while my website was quiet, my LeetCode account was still alive and kicking.

You can follow along here:

- **[LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/u/md_owais_/)** — where the actual struggle happens.
- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohd-owais-45a1a0324)** — where I share my daily POTD progress.

## The Winter Arc Was Never Just About DSA

I also wanted to work on my fitness, build projects, improve my skills, and become more disciplined.

Basically, I wanted to become a software engineer, a gym beast, and a productivity machine simultaneously.

Small problem: I'm still human, and the day still has only 24 hours.

Balancing college, coding, workouts, and everything else isn't as easy as making a perfect timetable at 2 AM and believing that tomorrow you'll become a completely different person.

We all know how that usually goes.

I'm learning to adjust my expectations, focus on what matters, and keep moving instead of feeling guilty whenever everything doesn't go according to plan.

## And Here Comes Another Boss Fight

My term-end examinations are approaching in the next 4–5 days.

Because apparently, CAT 2 wasn't enough character development for one semester.

For now, academics will need more attention again. I'll continue practising DSA and working on my other goals whenever possible, but I'm not going to promise a routine that even I know will be difficult to maintain during exam week.

The Winter Arc isn't cancelled. It's just experiencing some unexpected server downtime.

## What's Next?

I'll keep solving problems, posting my daily POTD on LinkedIn, working on my fitness, and improving my skills alongside college.

I may not always post regularly, but I don't want a gap between updates to become a reason to abandon the journey.

The goal was never to have a perfect transformation story. It was to start, learn, make mistakes, and gradually improve.

There will be productive days, unproductive days, and days when opening the syllabus feels like opening a final boss's health bar.

That's college life, I guess.

**Winter Arc status:** Still running.

**Productivity status:** Depends on the syllabus.

**Mental stability:** We'll discuss that after term-end exams.

See you in the next update. Hopefully, with fewer exams and more progress.
